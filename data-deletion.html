Data Deletion Instructions

Our Games do not collect personal information. The only data they store is your game progress (levels, stars, scores and settings), saved in Facebook's Instant Games player data and in your browser.

To delete it

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Games), find the game, and choose Remove. Tick the option to delete the data the game has saved, if it is offered.
  2. To clear what is saved in your browser, clear the site data for the game in your browser settings.
  3. Or email guruprasadreddykaditam@gmail.com with the name of the game and we will help you remove your data.

See also our Privacy Policy.
