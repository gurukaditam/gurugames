Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy applies to the casual puzzle games published by Guru Games on Facebook Instant Games: Color Flood 3D, Path Painter 3D, Word Morph 3D, Shadow Match 3D, Sort Stack 3D, Emoji Story 3D, Pipe Dream 3D, Memory Mosaic 3D, Letter Gravity 3D and Logic Locks 3D (the "Games").

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details. The Games run inside Facebook and do not use Facebook Login permissions beyond what Facebook Instant Games provides by default.

Game progress

Your progress (levels completed, stars, scores, settings such as sound on or off) is saved through the Facebook Instant Games player-data feature, so it stays with your Facebook account on Facebook's servers, and in your browser's local storage on your device. We use it only to restore your progress and show your own score.

Advertising

The Games show ads provided by Meta Audience Network. Ads are shown between levels, and rewarded ads are shown only if you choose to watch one for a hint or an undo. Meta decides which ads are shown, using its own policies and settings; see Meta's Privacy Policy and your Facebook ad preferences. We do not receive your personal data from these ads.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than Facebook / Meta, which provides the platform and the ads.

Deleting your data

See our data deletion instructions.
